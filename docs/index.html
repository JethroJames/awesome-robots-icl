From evidence to action

Learning at
the moment of use.

Demonstrations and interaction give a robot context for a new task. In-context learning turns that evidence into action, with neural parameters held fixed during deployment.

This survey connects the methods that make context useful to the challenges of physical transfer, memory, and evaluation.
